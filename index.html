เจตนา
jèt · thà · naa  ·  Thai for "intention"

This app is named after a word my mom carried with her — เจตนา. She taught me to be intentional about everything I do, to be a good person first, and to trust that the rest would follow.

Growing up, I saw firsthand how quietly money stress can affect a family. So in college, I made a decision: I wasn't going to let that be my story. I started tracking my money — not because someone taught me, but because I had to figure it out on my own.

It's what helped me save for my first trip abroad — an internship in Thailand, the country my mom carried with her long before I ever got to see it. Going there felt like her will becoming mine.

I built Jettana for anyone who feels the way I did — overwhelmed, unsure where to start, and never taught how any of this works. You just need a place to begin. This is that place.

— Maya 💚
