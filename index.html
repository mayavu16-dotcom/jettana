Every meaningful change begins with intention.
In Thai, that word is —

เจตนา
jèt · thà · naa

This app is named after a word my mom carried with her — เจตนา. She taught me that who you become matters more than what you have. To be intentional in the way you live, to choose kindness first, and to trust that everything else would follow.

Growing up, I watched my family carry the quiet weight of financial stress. From an early age, I promised myself I would learn what no one had taught us — not just to change my future, but one day to help others change theirs.

Those small, intentional choices eventually led me to Thailand, the home my mom left and never had the chance to return to. Standing there for the first time, I realized this journey had never been mine alone.

That's why I built Jettana. Not simply to help people budget — but to help them live with intention.

Because meaningful change doesn't happen all at once. It begins with one small, intentional decision.

You just need a place to begin. This is that place.

For my mom, who taught me the meaning of intention.
For you, who are choosing to begin.

— Maya 💚
